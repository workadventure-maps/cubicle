Cubicles

A falling-block versus game for the Super Nintendo, made for the WorkAdventure arcade. 1 to 4 players, in your browser.

How to play

Press Start to join. Alone, it's an endless score attack; a second player's Start turns it into a versus match. Clearing 2 or more lines at once sends junk rows to every opponent, the pieces fall faster every 30 seconds, and the last well standing wins. A player who doesn't touch the pad during a whole match sits out the next one.

Controls

Player 1←→ move, ↓ faster, ↑ or Z turn, X drop, Enter join
Player 2AD move, S faster, W turn, Space drop, Q join
GamepadsThe first gamepad is player 1, the second player 2, and so on up to 4. Up, A and X turn, B and Y drop.

Download

The ROM runs on any SNES emulator. For players 2 to 4, set port 2 to "Multitap".
